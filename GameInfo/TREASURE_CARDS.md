# Derelict Run: Treasure Cards (Salvage Deck)

The Salvage deck is the game's treasure deck. It holds 60 cards, one copy of each. Rules are in [RULES.md](RULES.md).

| Type | Cards |
|---|---|
| Gear | 34 |
| One-Shot | 16 |
| Promotion | 6 |
| Trick | 4 |
| **Total** | **60** |

## Promotions

Play a Promotion on your turn, outside combat, unless a card allows otherwise. A Promotion cannot raise a player to Rank 10.

| ID | Name | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| S051 | Field Commendation | Gain 1 Rank |
| S052 | Forged Service Record | Gain 1 Rank |
| S053 | Salvage Rights Deed | Gain 1 Rank |
| S054 | Hazard Pay Bonus | Gain 1 Rank and 2 Scrap |
| S055 | Guild Sponsorship | Gain 1 Rank if you are the lowest Rank or tied for lowest, otherwise gain 5 Scrap |
| S056 | Veteran Clearance | Gain 1 Rank and 1 Charge |
