# Derelict Run: Door Cards (Hatch Deck)

The Hatch deck is the game's door deck. It holds 80 cards. Some entries have multiple copies, listed in the Count column. Rules are in [RULES.md](RULES.md).

| Type | Entries | Cards |

| Threat | 34 | 34 |
| Hazard | 14 | 14 |
| Role | 6 | 12 |
| Origin | 5 | 10 |
| Modifier | 6 | 6 |
| Special | 4 | 4 |
| **Total** | **69** | **80** |
